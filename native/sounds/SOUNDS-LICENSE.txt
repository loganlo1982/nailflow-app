(lệnh 569, 21/10/2026) GIỌNG ĐỌC / VOICE
- No recorded or third-party voice files are shipped. The sentences ("Nancy ơi, bạn có khách…") are spoken by the phone's OWN system voice:
  · iPhone: AVSpeechSynthesizer (Apple's built-in voices, part of iOS, licensed to apps for speech output on the device) – the app writes the
    spoken sentence + a chime it synthesizes itself (two sine tones, made in code, no source file) to Library/Sounds/nfv_<id>.caf on that phone only.
  · Android: android.speech.tts.TextToSpeech (the phone's installed TTS engine, e.g. Google Speech Services), spoken live, nothing stored.
- Chime before the sentence on Android: nf_ping.ogg (made by NailFlow, CC0 – see above).
